## 📊 THE 5 CORE TOOLS AT A GLANCE

### 1️⃣ Customer Health Score
**Purpose**: Predict churn 60-90 days before it happens
- Multi-factor scoring (adoption, sentiment, payment, velocity)
- Risk levels: 0-100 scale
- Action: Identify at-risk customers for proactive intervention
- **Impact**: 15-25% churn reduction

| Metric | Input | Output |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Feature Adoption | % of features used | 0-100 score |
| Support Sentiment | CSAT from tickets | 0-100 score |
| Payment Health | Payment history | 0-100 score |
| Engagement Velocity | Usage growth MoM | 0-100 score |
| **Final Score** | Weighted average | **0-100** |

---

### 2️⃣ NRR Calculator ⭐ THE PRIMARY METRIC
**Purpose**: Measure true business health and growth
- Formula: (Beginning MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Beginning MRR × 100
- Benchmarks: <100% (declining), 100-110% (healthy), 110-120% (excellent), >120% (exceptional)
- Action: Track monthly, use for board reporting
- **Impact**: Visibility into sustainable growth

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Beginning MRR | Starting revenue | - |
| Expansion | Upsells + cross-sells | As high as possible |
| Churn | Lost revenue | <5% |
| **NRR %** | **((Beg + Exp - Churn) / Beg) × 100** | **>110%** |

---

### 3️⃣ Churn & Retention Analysis
**Purpose**: Understand customer economics and long-term viability
- Cohort retention curves (track by signup month)
- CLV = Customer Lifetime Value
- Payback = Months to recover acquisition cost
- Action: Validate business model, inform pricing
- **Impact**: Know if business is sustainable

| Metric | Formula | Decision |
|--------|---------|----------|
| CLV | (ARPU × Lifespan × Margin) - CAC | $5K+ is good |
| Payback | CAC / Monthly Profit | <12mo is healthy |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | CLV / CAC | 3:1 is target |
| Retention (12mo) | % retained after 1 year | 80%+ is excellent |

---

### 4️⃣ Customer Segmentation
**Purpose**: Allocate CS resources to maximize ROI
- RFM Analysis (9 segments: Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, Lost, etc.)
- Profit-based quadrants (High-value, Growth, Maintenance, Problem)
- Segment playbooks and strategies
- Action: Tailor approach per segment
- **Impact**: 20-30% productivity increase

| Segment | % Revenue | % Customers | CS Strategy |
|---------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| High-Value | 30% | 5% | Dedicated AM, monthly reviews |
| Growth | 40% | 15% | QBR, expansion focus |
| Maintenance | 25% | 50% | Self-service, group training |
| Problem | 5% | 30% | Triage only, consider exit |

---

### 5️⃣ Escalation Tracker
**Purpose**: Ensure operational excellence and SLA compliance
- SLA definitions by priority level (P1: 4hrs, P2: 24hrs, P3: 72hrs, P4: 30days)
- Real-time compliance monitoring
- Escalation workflows and triggers
- Action: Prevent churn from poor support
- **Impact**: 95%+ SLA compliance

| Priority | Response | Resolution | Escalation |
|----------|----------|-----------|------------|
| P1 (Critical) | <1 hour | <4 hours | Auto to engineering |
| P2 (High) | <4 hours | <24 hours | Standard queue |
| P3 (Medium) | <8 hours | <72 hours | As needed |
| P4 (Low) | <24 hours | <30 days | Product backlog |
