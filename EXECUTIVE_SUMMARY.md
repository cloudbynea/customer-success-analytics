## The 5 Tools Explained (CEO/Board Level)

### 1. Customer Health Score (0-100)

**What it shows:** Which customers are at risk?

| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 80+ | ✅ Healthy | Nurture for expansion |
| 60-79 | ⚠️ At-Risk | QBR + outreach |
| <60 | 🔴 Critical | Executive call needed |

**Business Impact:**
- Predict churn 60-90 days early
- Intervene before customer leaves
- Typical: 15-25% reduction in churn

**Expected Finding:**
- 60% of customers healthy
- 30% at-risk (recoverable with attention)
- 10% critical (needs intervention)

---

### 2. NRR Calculator (THE PRIMARY METRIC) ⭐

**What it shows:** Are we growing revenue from existing customers?

```
Formula: (Beginning MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Beginning MRR × 100

Benchmarks:
- <100% = Declining (problem!)
- 100-110% = Healthy (can grow)
- 110-120% = Excellent (scaling)
- >120% = Exceptional (rare)
```

**Business Impact:**
- Shows true health (new sales hide problems)
- Predictor of future revenue
- What VCs look at first

**Action by Level:**
- <100%: Emergency mode (hiring, retention focus)
- 100-110%: Growth focus (expand CS, sales)
- 110%+: Scale mode (invest aggressively)

---

### 3. Churn & Retention (Economics Check)

**What it shows:** Is our business model sustainable?

```
Three key metrics:

CLV = How much profit per customer
├─ Our CLV: $5,000
├─ Our CAC: $1,500
├─ Ratio: 3.3:1 ✅ Healthy

Payback = How many months to recover CAC
├─ Our payback: 12 months
├─ Target: <12 months ✅

Retention = Long-term customer viability
├─ Year 1 retention: 90%
├─ Year 3 retention: 65%
├─ Status: Good but needs improvement
```

**Business Impact:**
- Know if business model works
- Justifies sales/marketing spend
- Guides pricing strategy

---

### 4. Customer Segmentation (Resource Allocation)

**What it shows:** Which customers deserve which level of CS attention?

```
Four customer groups:

HIGH-VALUE (30% of MRR)
├─ Dedicated account manager
├─ Monthly executive reviews
├─ Custom solutions
└─ Budget: 30% of CS team

GROWTH (40% of MRR)
├─ Quarterly business reviews
├─ Playbook-based engagement
├─ Expansion focus
└─ Budget: 35% of CS team

MAINTENANCE (25% of MRR)
├─ Self-service + group training
├─ Community support
├─ Minimize costs
└─ Budget: 25% of CS team

PROBLEM (<5% of MRR)
├─ Triage only
├─ Consider exiting
├─ Don't waste resources
└─ Budget: 10% of CS team
```

**Business Impact:**
- Max ROI on CS investment
- 10x better than one-size-fits-all
- Enables scaling without hiring
